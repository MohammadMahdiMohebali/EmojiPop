How to Play

Click happy emojis (😃) to lose 1 point and turn them into angry emojis (😠).

Click angry emojis (😠) to score 1 point and turn them back into happy emojis (😃).

Click evil emojis (😈) to turn them back into happy emojis, but you don't score any points.

Your goal is to keep the number of evil emojis below 3.
